contract - hợp đồng - We signed a new contract today.
abide by - tuân thủ - You must abide by the terms of the agreement.
agreement - sự thỏa thuận - They reached a mutual agreement.
assurance - sự bảo đảm - He gave me his assurance that the project would be finished on time.
determine - quyết định / xác định - The court will determine the outcome of the case.
engage - tham gia - We want to engage more citizens in the process.
establish - thiết lập - They established a new business in 2020.
obligate - bắt buộc - You are obligated to pay your taxes.
party - bên (trong hợp đồng) - The two parties signed the document.
resolve - giải quyết - We need to resolve this issue quickly.
specific - cụ thể - Please be specific about your requirements.
provision - điều khoản - The provisions of the contract are very clear.
